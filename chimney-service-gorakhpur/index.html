Why does a kitchen chimney need regular cleaning?

Over time, cooking produces grease and smoke particles that accumulate inside the chimney — on filters, fan blades, and in the duct. This buildup reduces suction performance, makes the chimney work harder, increases noise, and can shorten the life of the motor. A clean chimney also reduces the fire risk from grease accumulation.

Most households cooking regularly should get professional chimney cleaning every 6 to 12 months. If you cook over a high flame frequently, cleaning every 3–6 months keeps performance consistent.

Self-Check Guide

Common Chimney Problems — What You Can Check

Safe basic checks you can do before calling a technician. Do not attempt to open or repair electrical components yourself.

💨 Chimney Suction Feels Weak
●
Check if the filter is visibly clogged with grease — this is the most common cause of low suction.
●
Ensure the chimney is set to the highest speed setting when cooking heavily.
●
If the chimney is a ducted type, check that the duct outlet is not blocked externally.
●
If suction is still low after filter cleaning, the motor or fan may need professional attention.
If suction doesn't improve, call OjasCare for a free doorstep inspection.
🔊 Chimney Making Noise
●
A rattling or vibrating sound can sometimes be caused by a loose chimney mounting — check if the chimney is firmly attached.
●
A dirty or clogged filter sometimes causes increased noise — try cleaning it.
●
Persistent grinding, humming, or squealing sounds usually indicate a motor or fan issue that needs a technician.
Persistent noise after basic checks — call OjasCare for diagnosis.
⚡ Chimney Not Turning On
●
Check the power socket — test it with another appliance to confirm the socket is working.
●
Check the main electrical circuit breaker — a tripped breaker can cut power to the chimney.
●
If touch controls aren't responding, the control panel or wiring may need professional repair.
No power after basic checks — call OjasCare. Do not attempt internal electrical repair yourself.
🫧 When to Get Chimney Cleaned
●
Suction is noticeably weaker than usual even at high speed.
●
Smoke does not clear from the kitchen as quickly as it used to.
●
Grease is dripping from the chimney body or filters appear visibly black and heavy.
●
The chimney hasn't been cleaned professionally in over a year.
●
Unusual smell coming from the chimney during operation.
