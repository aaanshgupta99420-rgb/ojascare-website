Refrigerator problems we repair in Gorakhpur

Most refrigerator faults start small: weaker cooling, a new noise, a little more frost. Hot summers and voltage swings put extra load on the compressor and the control board, so it pays to act early. Find your symptom below to see what our technician checks first.

  • Not cooling or not freezing

    We check the gas level and any leak, whether the compressor starts and keeps running, the condenser and fan motor, the thermostat or temperature sensor, and the control board.

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

  • Freezer is cold but the fridge section is warm

    This usually points to airflow. We check the evaporator and its fan motor, the defrost system, and any ice blocking the vents.

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

  • Ice buildup or heavy frost

    We check the defrost heater and sensor, the door gasket seal and the drain, then clear the ice properly.

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

  • Water leaking inside or onto the floor

    A blocked drain or a damaged gasket is often the cause. We clear the drain line, check the defrost drain and test the door seal.

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

  • Strange noise or vibration

    We check the fan motor, the compressor and its mounts, the condenser fan, and anything loose or touching the body.

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

  • Not starting, or no power

    We start with the power supply and wiring, then check the control board, the inverter board and the compressor start components.

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

  • High electricity bill or running non-stop

    We check the door seal, thermostat and sensor, gas level, a dirty condenser and how well the compressor is performing.

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

  • Door not closing, torn gasket, light or sensor not working

    We replace worn door gaskets and check the hinges, door switch, light, sensors and wiring.

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

  • Compressor problem or gas leakage

    Compressor repair or replacement, and gas leak repair with gas charging, are done at your home. Parts and gas are charged separately, and only after you approve the price.

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

Safety first: if you see sparks, smell burning, or the plug or socket feels hot, switch off at the mains and call us for emergency service. Do not keep trying to run the refrigerator.

What to check before you call

These quick checks sometimes solve the problem, and they help our technician find the fault faster if they do not.

Five quick checks

  • The plug and socket have power. Try another appliance in the same socket.
  • The temperature knob or display has not been moved to the warmest or off setting.
  • Both doors close fully and nothing is holding them open. The rubber gasket should not be torn.
  • There is some space behind and beside the refrigerator so heat can escape.
  • The vents inside are not blocked by packed food or ice.

While you wait for the technician

  • Keep the doors closed as much as possible so the cold stays in.
  • Move milk, medicines and other perishables to a cooler with ice if cooling has stopped for hours.
  • Tell us the brand, the type (single-door, double-door and so on) and what you noticed first. It helps us come prepared.
